# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project discussions should be respectful, specific, and focused on the work. Participants are expected to:

- welcome questions and constructive review;
- distinguish evidence from interpretation;
- give credit for code, data, experiments, and ideas;
- respect privacy and requests for confidentiality;
- accept correction and revise claims when evidence changes.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without permission is also not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in a public setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through a private contact method shown on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private route is available, open a short issue requesting private contact without describing sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently limit participation when needed to protect the project community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
